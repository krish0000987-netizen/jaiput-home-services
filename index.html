Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our home care services in Jaipur.

Do your cleaning technicians bring their own machines and chemicals?
Yes! Our team arrives fully equipped with heavy-duty industrial vacuum cleaners, single-disc floor scrubbing machines, high-pressure jet cleaners, steamers, ladders, microfiber wipers, and hospital-grade eco-friendly cleaning solutions. You only need to provide water and electricity.
How long does a complete villa or apartment deep cleaning take?
A standard 2BHK or 3BHK flat deep cleaning takes approximately 4 to 6 hours with a dedicated team of 3-4 professional cleaners. Larger duplexes and villas may take 6 to 8 hours to ensure spotless perfection.
Are the chemicals used safe for children, elderly, and pets?
Absolutely. We strictly avoid toxic or corrosive acids. All our chemicals are pH-balanced, biodegradable, and certified safe for residences with babies, elderly family members, and pets.
What type of stones can be polished and rubbed?
We polish all varieties including Italian Marble (Statuario, Botticino, Dyna), Indian White & Green Marble, Granite, Kota Stone, Terrazzo, and Mosaic tiles using multi-grit diamond pads and high-gloss crystallization.
How can I book a service and pay?
You can book instantly by calling 8875 198384 or clicking the WhatsApp button. You can pay conveniently via UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cash, or Bank Transfer *after* inspecting our work and being 100% satisfied.
